# Alex & Doodle's Big Adventure

A Zelda-flavored companion to Zoe's Sparkle Adventure, made for Alex (7). Play as Alex, with Doodle the orange-and-white tabby following along. There are no enemies, no fail state and no game over.

Play: https://howibrettyourmother.github.io/alex-games/adventure/

- The same familiar places as Zoe's game: Dad's House, Mom's Apartment, Meadow Brook (in place of KiddieKlub), the Park, Grandma Gail's, Upland Hills Farm, Raintree Park, Stony Creek, Downtown Rochester, plus trips to Florida, Colorado and a Michigan campout.
- Treasure chests and gentle light puzzles in each place (push a crate, step on stones in order, find a key, or a little counting puzzle at school). Wrong answers just say "try again."
- Helper quests for family and friends, hidden treasure stars, a cape, a dino hood and an explorer hat.
- iPad landscape first, big on-screen controls, offline after the first visit, progress saved on the device.

Built from the Doodle & Zoe adventure engine, with a new save key (`adba_`) so Zoe's progress is untouched.
