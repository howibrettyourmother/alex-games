# Credits

Alex & Doodle's Big Adventure is an original kids' game for Alex, built on the Doodle & Zoe Sparkle Adventure engine.

- Characters and places are family originals (cartoon drawings only; no photographs of people).
- Doodle is a drawn orange-and-white tabby based on the family's cat.
- Speech is recorded offline with Kokoro TTS (not the browser's speechSynthesis).
- Music is original chiptune themes written for the adventure engine.
- Hold-to-go-home, audio guard, and offline service worker follow the other games on Alex's hub.
